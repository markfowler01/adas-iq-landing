What it does

One platform for the whole shop.

Not a scrubber with some extra screens. ADAS IQ is an operating platform — built around how mobile ADAS companies actually work.

Job Board

Every open job, one screen. Assign techs, track status, and see what's coming. Updates live.

CRM + Shop Profiles

Every shop you work with, their billing rules, contact info, and job history — always current.

Dispatch + Phones

Calls and texts routed to the right person. Job alerts by SMS. Technician assignment in one tap.

Auto Billing

Billing rules per shop and insurer. Invoices built from job data — not from memory.

Estimate Scrubbing

CCC and carrier estimates reviewed for missing ADAS lines. Right procedures, every time.

Documentation

Required photos, calibration reports, and OEM docs attached to every job. No chasing at invoice time.
